§ Semiconductor Industry

The foundry landscape continues its relentless march towards atomic-scale precision, with TSMC's 2nm (N2) process node emerging as the next critical inflection point. As the world's leading pure-play foundry, TSMC's N2 is slated for mass production in the second half of 2025, marking their decisive transition from FinFET to Gate-All-Around (GAA) nanosheet transistors. This architectural shift, where the gate fully surrounds the channel, is fundamental to mitigating short-channel effects and improving electrostatic control, crucial for sub-3nm scaling.
