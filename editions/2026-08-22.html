TSMC continues to push the absolute boundaries of physics and engineering with its next-generation process nodes. Their upcoming 2nm N2 process, slated for risk production by the end of 2024 and mass production in the second half of 2025, marks a pivotal shift. N2 is TSMC's inaugural node to fully adopt Gate-All-Around (GAA) nanosheet transistors. Unlike FinFETs, where the gate only surrounds three sides of the channel, GAAFETs wrap the gate completely around the channel.
